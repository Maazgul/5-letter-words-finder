Best Starting Words for Wordle

Many Wordle experts and data scientists suggest starting with words that have common vowels (A, E, I, O, U) and common consonants (R, S, T, L, N).

Best starting words: CRANE, SLATE, TRACE, ROAST, CRATE, SLANT, ADIEU, AUDIO, RAISE, SOARE.

Using these words in your first attempt helps you eliminate 5 common letters fast, and you can guess the correct word in 2nd or 3rd attempt.
