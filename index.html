What is a 5 Letter Words Finder? How to Use it for Wordle & Scrabble

A 5 Letter Words Finder is a powerful tool for Wordle players, Scrabble lovers, and crossword solvers. If you are stuck in today's Wordle puzzle, this tool helps you find all possible 5 letter words based on the letters you know. It saves your time and improves your winning streak.

How to use this tool?

1. Starts With: If you know the first letter of the word, for example your word starts with 'S', enter S in the first box.
2. Ends With: If you know the last letter, enter it in Ends With box.
3. Contains: If you know some letters are in the word but you don't know the exact position, enter them in Contains box. For example, if you know A and R are in the word.
4. Click Search and our tool will instantly filter 12,000+ words and show you results in beautiful tags. You can click any word to copy it.

Best Starting Words for Wordle

Many Wordle experts and data scientists suggest starting with words that have common vowels (A, E, I, O, U) and common consonants (R, S, T, L, N). The best starting words are CRANE, SLATE, TRACE, ROAST, CRATE, SLANT, CRATE. Using these words in your first attempt helps you eliminate 5 common letters fast and you can guess the correct word in 2nd or 3rd attempt.
